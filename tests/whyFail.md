# I get a genre of music
I was listening "Swing Lynn" by Harmless. It is essentially this verse repeated three
times and then some viby words with music:


```
Hey, there, little honey, won't you groove?
I've been trying all night to dance with you
Hey, there, little lonely, won't you stay?
I said, "I would rather die than feel this pain"
You said, "I know I feel very much the same
But I am afraid that I don't know, knowing is not my thing"
But I would like to dance with you
Awkwardly in haze
To this little tune
```
I realized, this is a way to elevate poetry. You right a musical ensemble that represents
the mood. This is vibe music, you don't need a conclusion you just need a feeling captured
in as a snapshot in time and space.
